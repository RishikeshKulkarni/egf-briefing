01Org snapshot

EGF at a glance

EGF is the UAE's flagship federal growth-equity platform — about 18 months old (launched May 2025). AED 1B mandate, backed by EDB (Emirates Development Bank). Active minority investor in SMEs across four national-priority sectors. Three announced investments to date: Tarmeem (healthcare), CarniStore (food) and FMSi (fleet-management tech, Sep 2026 — its first co-investment). The thesis is the "missing middle": companies too big for VC, too entrepreneurial for traditional PE.

The five value-creation pillars (EGF's own framing)

  1. Strategic guidance — board-level direction and thesis sharpening
  2. Institutionalization & governance — turning founder-led companies into board-led ones
  3. Operational excellence — KPIs, finance function, cadence
  4. Market access — opening doors via the EDB and federal network
  5. Talent access — placing senior operators into portfolio companies
Anchor

EGF isn't a generic growth fund — it is an industrial-policy instrument. Operation 300bn (the UAE's strategy to grow industrial GDP to AED 300bn by 2031) and the ICV (In-Country Value) programme are upstream. Everything it does ladders up to that, not to "returns" in isolation. The full strategy map is in §04.

Read

Three deals in 18 months is deliberate, not slow. At AED 10–50M tickets and a five-pillar value-creation model that demands hands-on engagement, a young Portfolio Operations team can responsibly absorb maybe 4–6 actives early on. The discipline is the story — and co-investing alongside a lead like Shorooq (FMSi) is how EGF adds deal flow without overloading its own team.

04The macro EGF ladders to

National strategies EGF plugs into

EGF doesn't do growth equity in a vacuum. Every portfolio company ladders up to a specific federal strategy with a named target year and named owner ministry. The map explains why the fund exists — not just what it does.

Operation 300bnMoIAT · target 2031

What it is: Grow industrial sector contribution to UAE GDP from AED 133bn to AED 300bn by 2031; build 13,500 industrial SMEs; raise industrial R&D to 2% of GDP. EDB has allocated AED 30bn over five years as the financing arm.

EGF's role: The federal growth-equity instrument for this. Sits underneath EDB. The NCP is the explicit funnel.

NCPIndustrial Resilience AgreementFuture manufacturing portfolio companies
National Food Security Strategy 2051Ministry of Climate Change & Env · target 2051

What it is: UAE #1 on the Global Food Security Index by 2051. Five objectives — diversify supply sources, expand local production (smart farming, hydroponics, vertical farming), reduce food waste, strengthen value chain, mitigate risk.

EGF's role: Equity into private-sector food champions building the local production base. CarniStore is the inaugural deal under this strategy.

CarniStore
"We the UAE 2031" — HealthcareFederal vision · target 2031

What it is: UAE in the global top 10 for healthcare quality by 2031. Healthcare-services localization. Federal Decree-Law 38/2024 consolidated drug regulation to attract R&D and biotech investment.

EGF's role: Equity into domestic specialty providers reducing outbound medical tourism. Tarmeem is the wedge.

Tarmeem
ICV (In-Country Value) programmeADNOC · ongoing

What it is: ADNOC's localization mandate — routes procurement spend toward UAE-based suppliers, scores them on local-value contribution. The largest single offtake/localization mechanism in the country.

EGF's role: The Industrial Resilience Agreement (signed at MIITE 2026, see §05) plugs portfolio manufacturing companies directly into ADNOC's supplier ecosystem. This is the single most valuable distribution channel EGF can offer a portfolio company.

Future manufacturing dealsNCP pipeline
UAE National AI Strategy 2031 + Agentic AI mandateFederal & Dubai · target 2028

What it is — federal: 23 April 2026 — UAE Cabinet directive that 50% of federal government services run on autonomous AI agents by 2028. What it is — Dubai: 4 May 2026 (the same day MIITE opened) — Crown Prince Sheikh Hamdan launched a two-year mandate for Dubai's entire private sector to adopt agentic AI, with training tracks via Dubai Chamber of Commerce, government-funded incubators, and dedicated investment funds.

EGF's role: No public EGF position as of Nov 2026. But CarniStore is Dubai-HQ — so portfolio companies are directly subject to the Dubai mandate. A new "AI readiness" lens almost certainly has to enter the value-creation playbook: due-diligence pre-investment, operational lever post-investment, board-level reporting after. FMSi's AI video telematics makes it concrete.

Pattern to notice

Every strategy has a target year, a named ministry/sponsor, and a measurable goal. That's the UAE policy idiom — "Operation 300bn, MoIAT, AED 300bn by 2031", not "the industrial plan".

06Background

The BGF (Business Growth Fund) lineage

EGF is structurally a UAE adaptation of the UK's BGF (Business Growth Fund), a £3bn growth-equity platform launched in 2011 by the major UK banks alongside the government. The evidence:

  • Stephen Welton — founder of BGF — sits on the EGF board.
  • Matthew Singh — EGF's CIO — came directly from BGF, where he led investments from 2015.
  • Same playbook: government/bank-backed, minority equity, "patient capital", growth-stage SMEs, sector-priority focus, governance-first value creation.
  • BGF is the world's most-active growth investor by deal volume — over 600 investments since 2011 — so the model is battle-tested.
07Glossary

Operating vocabulary

IRR (Internal Rate of Return)
Time-weighted return on invested capital. Optics-heavy; penalizes long holds.
MOIC (Multiple on Invested Capital)
Total return divided by cost. Patient-capital favourite.
TVPI (Total Value to Paid-In)
Includes unrealized value. The main reportable for an early-stage fund.
DPI (Distributions to Paid-In)
Cash actually returned to LPs. The only one that pays bills.
RVPI (Residual Value to Paid-In)
The "still in the ground" piece — TVPI minus DPI.
J-curve
The early dip in fund returns before exits start. EGF still lives here.
100-day plan
The post-close stabilize-and-set-cadence playbook for a newly acquired portfolio company.
Value bridge
Decomposition of equity-value uplift into revenue growth, margin, multiple expansion, debt paydown.
EBITDA bridge
Period-over-period walk of EBITDA: volume, price, mix, cost, one-offs.
Run-rate synergies
Annualized cost or revenue synergies achievable once a programme is fully implemented.
NWC (Net Working Capital)
Receivables + inventory − payables. The most under-managed value lever in growth-stage portfolio companies.
ICV (In-Country Value)
ADNOC's localization mandate. This is the gate to the Industrial Resilience Agreement.
NCP (National Champions Programme)
The EGF + MoIAT + MoET initiative launched at MIITE, May 2026.
Operation 300bn
UAE's strategy to grow industrial GDP to AED 300bn by 2031. The macro thesis EGF ladders to.
The "missing middle"
EGF's own term — SMEs too big for VC, too entrepreneurial for traditional PE.
Active minority
A minority equity stake with structured value-creation engagement. Governance via board seats and reporting rights, not control.
WIP (Work In Progress) limit
A cap on how many initiatives are active at once. The operating-system version of "fewer, bigger, better."
IC (Investment Committee)
The body that approves new deals. EGF's IC includes the CEO, CIO, CPO and board representation.
LP (Limited Partner)
A capital provider to a fund. For EGF, EDB is effectively the LP layer.
Co-investment
Investing alongside a lead investor who structures the deal. FMSi (Shorooq-led) is EGF's first.
Buy-and-build
Growing a platform company through bolt-on acquisitions as well as organic growth — the FMSi plan.
Agentic AI
AI systems that act autonomously — noticing issues, contacting parties, executing tasks — beyond chatbots or basic automation. Mandated for Dubai's private sector within two years (announced 4 May 2026).
